Mimamori 1.5.0 tells you more about what's still under way: agent cards show how long the turn and the session have run, and agents waiting on their subagents or background commands get a group of their own. The iPhone companion now reaches your Mac away from home over Tailscale, and has a demo to try without a Mac.

## Install or update

- **Already running Mimamori?** It offers this update itself (Mimamori › Check for Updates…).
- **New here?** Download **Mimamori-1.5.0.dmg** below, open it and drag **Mimamori** to **Applications**. It's signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Or run `brew install --cask cocoabeans/tap/mimamori`.

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, and `git`.

## How long, and what's still running

- **Turn and session clocks.** An agent card's timer restarts at each step, so it says how long the current step has taken. Cards now also show **turn 4m · session 1h 12m**: in the Agents window, in the main window's banner when you expand it, and on the iPhone. Between turns it reads "last turn 6m", and an ended session's clock stops when it ended.
- **Waiting on tasks.** An agent that's done with its own part can still have work going that it started. Those agents now get a **Waiting on tasks** group, between Working and Ready, in the Agents window, the menu bar's popover and on the iPhone:
  - one whose subagents are still running after it stopped ("working · waiting on 2 subagents"), which was under Working;
  - one that left commands running in the background when the turn became yours ("ready · 2 running in the background"), which was under Ready.

## Not Watched Yet, all at once

When agents work in several repositories Mimamori doesn't watch, the ✕ in the sidebar's **Not Watched Yet** header now dismisses them all: **Not Now** or **Don't Ask Again** for every one, instead of a menu per row.

## Your iPhone, away from home

For the iPhone companion you build onto your iPhone:

- **Over Tailscale.** With [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) on your Mac and your iPhone, signed in to the same tailnet, the iPhone reaches Mimamori from any network, cellular included. There's nothing to set up:
  - Mimamori keeps the port it listens on from one launch to the next, and tells your paired iPhone its tailnet address when the iPhone connects at home.
  - Away, when the iPhone can't find your Mac on its network, it connects over Tailscale, encrypted with the pairing's key as always. Its status line says **Over Tailscale**.
  - **Settings › iPhone** shows the address your iPhone uses, under **Away from this network**.
  - Your iPhone needs to connect to this version at home once to learn the address, or you can pair it again.
- **Try the Demo.** Without a Mac at hand, **Try the Demo** on the iPhone's pairing screen, or in its Mac menu, shows a sample Mac's agents. You can allow, deny and answer them, Face ID included, without anything leaving the iPhone.

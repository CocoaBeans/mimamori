Mimamori 1.6.0 points you to Mimamori Companion, now free on the App Store, and shows OMP agents waiting on a background job as waiting, not ready.

## Install or update

- **Already running Mimamori?** It offers this update itself (Mimamori › Check for Updates…).
- **New here?** Download **Mimamori-1.6.0.dmg** below, open it and drag **Mimamori** to **Applications**. It's signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Or run `brew install --cask cocoabeans/tap/mimamori`.

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, and `git`.

## Your iPhone

- **Get the iPhone App.** Mimamori Companion is on the App Store, free. **Get the iPhone App** in Settings › iPhone opens it there; install it, then pair from the same page. Building it yourself still adds staying connected in the background and the Live Activity.

## Fixes

- **OMP agents waiting on a background job.** When OMP moves a long command to the background and ends its turn to wait for it, the agent now waits under **Waiting on tasks**, "ready · 1 running in the background", with the job listed, until the job ends. Before, it read as ready for your prompt. Save the OMP extension again from Settings › Agents › Other Agents to get this.

Mimamori 1.5.1 tints agents waiting on their background work to match their group, and brings the iPhone companion a Live Activity, a background log, and fixes for both.

## Install or update

- **Already running Mimamori?** It offers this update itself (Mimamori › Check for Updates…).
- **New here?** Download **Mimamori-1.5.1.dmg** below, open it and drag **Mimamori** to **Applications**. It's signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Or run `brew install --cask cocoabeans/tap/mimamori`.

Requires macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, and `git`.

## Fixes

- **Waiting on tasks, in teal.** An agent whose own part is done while its subagents or background commands still run is listed under **Waiting on tasks**. Its card, timer and icon are now teal like the group, instead of keeping ready green or working blue. The same goes for the iPhone's cards.

## Your iPhone

For the iPhone companion you build onto your iPhone:

- **A Live Activity.** With **Stay connected in the background** on, your agents show on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on an Apple Watch: who needs you first, how many are working, and the most pressing agents with their timers. **Show agents on the Lock Screen**, in the iPhone's settings, turns it off.
  - It updates at once when who needs you changes, and otherwise at most every 30 seconds: sent with every step, iOS stopped taking its updates and it went stale.
- **Background Log.** In the iPhone's settings, what the app did while you weren't looking: when it came and went, stayed connected, lost touch with your Mac, each audio interruption, and the Live Activity's updates and whether iOS took them. If iOS stopped the app in the background, it says from when to when. It stays on the iPhone unless you share it.
- **Privacy Policy and Support** links in the iPhone's settings.
- **Compact cards fit.** A compact card no longer squeezes "26 tool calls this turn" into a column a word wide beside its clocks: when they don't fit, the clocks and changes go on a line below.
